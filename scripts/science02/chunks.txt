Build a car | in one minute.
Every car starts | as a giant roll | of flat steel.
One roll can weigh | twenty-five tons | and stretch | for over a mile.
Now press it | into shape.
A stamping press | slams down | with thousands | of tons of force.
In one hit, | a flat sheet | becomes a door, | a roof, or a hood.
Weld it together.
Hundreds of robots | fuse the panels | with thousands | of spot welds.
Each weld is | a burst of | electric current | that melts the steel | in a split second.
Now dip it | in paint.
The whole body sinks | into an electric bath, | and charged paint | crawls into | every hidden corner.
Then robots spray | primer, color, | and a glossy | clear coat.
All those layers | together are | only as thick | as a sheet of paper.
Now add | the heart.
The engine and axles | are lifted from below | and bolted on | in one move.
Factory workers | call this moment | the marriage.
Count the parts.
A single car | is made of about | thirty thousand parts, | down to | the smallest screw.
Now hose it down.
Every finished car | gets blasted with water | to hunt for | even the tiniest leak.
On a modern line, | a brand new car | rolls out | about every minute.
From a roll of steel | to a car | in about a day. | So grab some steel and…
