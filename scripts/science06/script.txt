> Feel your heartbeat.
Your heart beats about a hundred thousand times a day.
With each beat, it pushes about seventy milliliters of blood out into your body.
> Now breathe in.
Air rushes down your windpipe into two lungs, filled with about three hundred million tiny air sacs.
Those sacs are thinner than a sheet of paper, and that is where oxygen crosses into your blood.
> Send the signal.
Your brain sends electrical messages along nerves at over a hundred meters per second.
A single thought can reach your toes before you finish blinking.
> Now move your hand.
Muscles pull on bones through tendons, and each muscle can only pull, never push.
Your arm works with pairs of muscles, one pulls while the other relaxes.
> Fuel the machine.
Food gets broken down in your gut into sugar, which every cell burns to make energy.
Your body uses about two thousand calories a day, just to keep running.
> Clean up.
Your kidneys filter around one hundred eighty liters of blood every day.
Most of that is cleaned and sent back, and the rest leaves as urine.
