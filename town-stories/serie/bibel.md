# Series bible: "The 3 AM Letters"

**Format:** "In my town..." story in blocky 3D toy style (original characters, no brands), 9:16, 65 to 90 s, English.
**Platforms:** YouTube Shorts first. Later, compile episodes 1 to N into one long video of 8+ minutes (mid-roll ads).
**Hook rule:** The first sentence states the town's rule plus the problem.
**Ending:** Cliffhanger plus exactly 3 numbered options. "Comment 1, 2 or 3."
From episode 2 on, start with: "You decided: ..."

## World rule
Every night at 3 AM a letter appears in Pip's mailbox. Nobody else gets one.
The letter says what will happen tomorrow, and it always comes true.

## Characters (keep them identical in every episode!)
- **Pip** (narrator, about 9 years old): blocky toy figure. Head: skin tone #F2C29B with a short brown hair cap #5A3A22.
  Yellow hoodie #FFC928, blue trousers #3557C9, red sneakers #E23B3B. Big black eyes, small mouth.
- **Mr. Crumb** (baker): white apron, tall white chef's hat, round moustache.
- **Ms. Honk** (teacher): green cardigan, glasses. Sometimes she turns into a goose.
- **Grandma Ruby**: purple dress, grey hair bun, round glasses. "Knows secrets."
- **Mailbox**: red with a round lid and a little flag. Glows green at 3:00.

## Town
Small houses in pastel colours, cobblestone street, lanterns, forest at the edge of town, big moon.
