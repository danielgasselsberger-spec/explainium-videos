1. You decided. I followed the glowing footprints.
2. Into the forest. At three in the morning. In my pajamas. Great idea, everyone.
3. The footprints glowed blue. And they were small. Kid sized.
4. Then I noticed something. They looked exactly like my slippers.
5. Bunny slippers. With ears. Don't laugh.
6. An owl looked down at me and said: Whooo?
7. I said: Me. I think.
8. The footprints stopped in a clearing.
9. And right in the middle stood a mailbox. In the forest. Glowing green.
10. My name was written on it. Pip.
11. Inside were two letters.
12. The first one said: Hi Pip. It's me. You. From tomorrow.
13. Don't trust the letters from the town. They are lying.
14. My knees were shaking. Then I heard footsteps behind me.
15. Crunch. Crunch. Crunch.
16. And the footsteps were glowing blue.
17. Option one. Turn around.
18. Option two. Run home as fast as I can.
19. Option three. Open the second letter.
