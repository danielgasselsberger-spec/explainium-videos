# Episode 1: "Tomorrow, the whole town will disappear"

## Narration (voice: kid-like, fast, funny but tense)
1. In my town, every night at 3 A M, a letter appears in my mailbox.
2. Nobody else gets one. Just me.
3. And every letter tells me what will happen tomorrow.
4. Sounds cool, right? Trust me. It's not.
5. Monday's letter said: The bakery will explode.
6. With pancakes. Pancakes everywhere. Mister Crumb cried.
7. Tuesday's letter said: Your teacher will turn into a goose.
8. Honk. She still gave us homework.
9. Wednesday's letter said: It will rain spaghetti.
10. It did. My dog had the best day of his life.
11. So tonight, I stayed awake. Two fifty-nine.
12. Three o'clock. The mailbox started glowing. Green.
13. I ran outside in my pajamas and opened the letter.
14. It said: Tomorrow, the whole town will disappear. Except you.
15. My hands were shaking. Then I turned it around.
16. You can stop it. But you have to choose.
17. Option one. Follow the glowing footprints into the forest.
18. Option two. Wake up Grandma Ruby. She knows secrets.
19. Option three. Write a letter back.
20. Comment one, two, or three. The top comment decides what I do. Please. Don't let my town disappear!
