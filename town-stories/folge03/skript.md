## Narration
1. You decided. I ran home. Faster than ever. In bunny slippers.
2. Through the forest. The owl yelled: Whooo is running?
3. Behind me. Crunch. Crunch. Crunch. The glowing footsteps were following me.
4. Then I saw my house. All the lights were on.
5. Wait. I turned them off before I left.
6. I opened the front door. Very, very quietly.
7. The kitchen smelled like pancakes. At four in the morning.
8. And someone was sitting at our table. Eating my cereal.
9. Yellow hoodie. Brown hair. Bunny slippers.
10. It was me. Another me.
11. He looked up and said: Finally. You are late.
12. I screamed. He screamed. The cat screamed. We don't even have a cat.
13. Then the other me held up a letter. Number three.
14. He whispered: If you read this, you can never go back.
15. Outside, the footsteps stopped. Right in front of our door.
16. Knock. Knock. Knock.
17. Option one. Read letter number three.
18. Option two. Open the front door.
19. Option three. Hide under the table. With me.
20. Comment one, two, or three. Quick. They are knocking again!
